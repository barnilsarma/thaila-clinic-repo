ABOUT US

Our clinic was founded in 2020 by Gynecologist Dr. S. Shwetha with a clear mission—to offer specialised, high-quality healthcare in a setting that is simple, affordable, and patient-friendly.

We began with our first centre in Nanganallur and have now expanded to our second branch at Hasthinapuram, Chrompet, serving the community with dedication and trust.

Over the years, the clinic has grown into a multispeciality centre bringing together experienced doctors, advanced diagnostics, and 24/7 clinical support. Guided by a commitment to ethical practice, transparent care, and accessible specialist services, we continue to grow as a trusted healthcare destination for the community.

We are a multispeciality clinic dedicated to bridging the gap between patients and speciality doctors. With a team of trusted specialists, we make quality healthcare simple, affordable, and easily accessible for every family.

ABOUT DR. S. SHWETHA

Dr. Shwetha , Director of Sri Thaila clinic , Consultant in Obstetrics and Gynaecology, holding an MBBS, MD in Obstetrics and Gynaecology, a Diploma in Cosmetic Gynaecology, a Fellowship in Laparoscopy, and a Fellowship in Reproductive Medicine. She specializes in managing high-risk pregnancies, performing minimal invasive surgeries and laparoscopies, and providing expert care in menopause, cancer screening, gynaecologic oncology, and colposcopy. Her training in cosmetic gynaecology allows her to offer specialized care for women’s intimate health, combining aesthetics with functionality. A strong advocate for safe and natural vaginal deliveries, Dr. Shwetha guides mothers with personalized support throughout pregnancy, including exercise, nutrition, and lifestyle modifications to ensure a healthy pregnancy and delivery. She is also passionate about preventive oncology, offering comprehensive cancer screening and education to help women take proactive steps for their health. Committed to holistic, patient-centered care, Dr. Shwetha focuses on adolescent health, nutritional and lifestyle guidance for conditions like PCOD, and empowering women to make informed health decisions. Her expertise, coupled with a compassionate and approachable demeanor, makes her a trusted and reliable healthcare provider in Obstetrics and Gynaecology.
